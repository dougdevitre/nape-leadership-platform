Privacy Notice

What this prototype does with your data

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · This site is a prototype operated in connection with the National Association of Probation Executives (NAPE) executive leadership program under development.

Data that stays on your device

Your growth plan (goals and milestones), reflections, assessment answers and result, and saved profile details are stored only in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never transmitted to a server, and no one at NAPE can see them. This also means they are not backed up: clearing your browser data, using a different browser, or switching devices removes them. Use Export plan on the Growth Plan page to keep your own backup.

Data you send to NAPE

Information leaves your device only when you choose to send it, in two places. The interest form on the Connect page sends your name, email address, and (optionally) your role and agency, used solely to contact you about this program — announcements, cohort dates, and how to apply. The Feedback button sends the comment you write, the page it's about, and your name if you include it, used solely to improve this prototype. Neither is sold, and neither is shared with anyone outside NAPE and the service providers below.

Connections page

The Connections page loads NAPE's partner directory (organization names, categories, descriptions, and websites) through this site's server from Airtable. That request carries no information about you beyond the standard server logs described below. The campaigns you build, the drafts you edit, and your notes are stored only in this browser, like your growth plan. Messages are never sent by this site; you copy them into your own email.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts this site and, like most web hosts, keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations.
  • Airtable stores interest-form and feedback submissions on NAPE's behalf, and hosts the partner directory the Connections page reads.
  • Google Fonts serves this site's typefaces; when the pages load, your browser requests font files from Google's servers, which discloses your IP address to Google.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Removing your data

To remove everything this site has stored in this browser, use the button below. To have your interest-form submission deleted from NAPE's records, contact the NAPE office and it will be removed.

Children

This site is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

As a prototype, this site and this notice may change as the program develops. Material changes will be reflected in the date above.
