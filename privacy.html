Privacy Notice

What this prototype does with your data

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · This site is a prototype operated in connection with the National Association of Probation Executives (NAPE) executive leadership program under development.

Data that stays on your device

Your growth plan (goals and milestones), reflections, assessment answers and result, and saved profile details are stored only in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never transmitted to a server, and no one at NAPE can see them. This also means they are not backed up: clearing your browser data, using a different browser, or switching devices removes them. Use Export plan on the Growth Plan page to keep your own backup.

Data you send to NAPE

Information leaves your device only when you choose to send it, in four places. The interest form on the Connect page sends your name, email address, and (optionally) your role and agency, used solely to contact you about this program — announcements, cohort dates, and how to apply. The Feedback button sends the comment you write, the page it's about, and your name if you include it, used solely to improve this prototype. The partner-connection tool has an optional, per-campaign "Share this campaign's progress with NAPE" switch, off by default; when you turn it on, NAPE receives your name, role, and agency, the partner organization and the kind of role you're reaching, your goal, how many steps you've completed, the status you set, your outcome note if you write one, and your email address if you had already joined the interest list on that device. It never receives your contact's name, your message drafts, or your step notes. NAPE uses this only to understand which partnerships are forming and to support members. Turning it off stops further updates; what was already sent stays with NAPE unless you ask the NAPE office to remove it. The Media page has an optional "Tell NAPE what you published" panel, off by default; when you use it, NAPE receives the resource you promoted and its category, your stated goal, audience, and shared-goal line, the channel (and platform and post link if you provide one), and your name and agency if you entered them. NAPE uses this only to reshare member content from official channels and to understand what members are sharing; it can be removed on request like the rest. None of this is sold, and none of it is shared with anyone outside NAPE and the service providers below.

Partner-connection tool

The partner-connection tool on the Connect page loads NAPE's partner directory (organization names, categories, descriptions, and websites) through this site's server from Airtable. That request carries no information about you beyond the standard server logs described below. The campaigns you build, the drafts you edit, and your notes are stored only in this browser, like your growth plan. Messages are never sent by this site; you copy them into your own email. Progress summaries go to NAPE only if you turn on sharing for a campaign, as described above. The Media page works the same way: it reads the same directory, everything you generate (prompts, posts, images, one-pagers) is created in this browser, and nothing is sent unless you use its share panel.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts this site and, like most web hosts, keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations.
  • Airtable stores interest-form, feedback, opted-in partner-connection progress, and opted-in published-media submissions on NAPE's behalf, and hosts the partner directory the Connect and Media pages read.
  • Google Fonts serves this site's typefaces; when the pages load, your browser requests font files from Google's servers, which discloses your IP address to Google.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Removing your data

To remove everything this site has stored in this browser, use the button below. To have your interest-form submission, shared partner-connection progress, or shared published-media note deleted from NAPE's records, contact the NAPE office and it will be removed.

Children

This site is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

As a prototype, this site and this notice may change as the program develops. Material changes will be reflected in the date above.
