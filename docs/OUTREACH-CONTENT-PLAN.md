# NAPE Executive Leadership Experience — Outreach, Scripts & Design Asset Plan

**Status:** Draft for NAPE review · **Owner:** TBD · **Last updated:** 2026-09-08

---

## Answer first

The platform is built; the **promotion layer around it is not**. Today the site can
explain the program to someone who already found it, but there is nothing that:

1. **Gets a chief to the site** (no outreach copy, no email sequences, no affiliate/chapter kit)
2. **Lets a champion carry it for us** (no talk tracks, no one-pager, no slides)
3. **Survives a share** (no OG image — a LinkedIn or email paste renders as a blank card)
4. **Tells us what worked** (no UTM capture, no referral-source field, no analytics)

This plan closes those four gaps with **34 concrete assets across 4 workstreams**,
sequenced into three phases over ~10 weeks. Phase 1 (2 weeks, 9 assets) is the
minimum viable promotion kit and is the recommended starting point.

### ASSUMPTIONS (confirm before Phase 1 ships)

- **A1.** "NAPE" = the national probation-executive association referenced in the
  repo README; the program is Board-approved for *development*, not yet launched.
  All external copy therefore stays in "coming soon / in development" tense.
- **A2.** No cohort dates, tuition, curriculum hours, CEU credits, or faculty names
  are final. No asset states any of these until NAPE confirms them in writing.
- **A3.** Distribution channels available: NAPE member email list, NAPE website,
  NAPE LinkedIn, state/regional affiliate chapters, and NAPE's annual conference
  or training institute presence.
- **A4.** Primary conversion event = interest-form submission at `/connect`.
  Secondary = assessment completion at `/journey`.
- **A5.** NAPE staff/volunteers, not an agency, produce these. Every asset spec
  below is designed to be built in Canva or in-repo HTML — no design software required.

## 1. Audiences and what each one needs

| # | Audience | What they care about | Primary ask | Primary asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Deputy chiefs / supervisors on deck** | "Am I ready for the chief's chair?" | Take the assessment, join the list | Assessment-first social + email |
| A2 | **Sitting chiefs / directors** | Succession, retaining their bench | Nominate a deputy; consider mentoring | Nomination email + mentor one-pager |
| A3 | **NAPE board & affiliate presidents** | Member value, association relevance | Announce it to their chapter | Ambassador toolkit + 5-min talk track |
| A4 | **Retired chiefs / potential mentors** | Legacy, giving back, low time cost | Volunteer as mentor/coach | Mentor recruitment script + FAQ |
| A5 | **Agency budget approvers (county admin, courts)** | ROI, retention, succession risk | Approve time/funds when launched | "Case for your agency" one-pager |
| A6 | **Partners / sponsors / foundations** | Field impact, visibility | Fund a cohort seat or a module | Sponsor prospectus |
| A7 | **Adjacent associations & media** (APPA, NIC, state COAs, field press) | Newsworthy, non-promotional | Run the announcement | Press/announcement kit |

**Segmentation note:** A1 is the volume audience. A2 and A3 are the *leverage*
audiences — one chief nominating three deputies outperforms 100 impressions.
Weight effort accordingly: ~50% of Phase 1 effort goes to A2/A3 enablement.

## 3. Workstream B — Scripts (10 assets)

Every script ships in the same structure: **Purpose · Audience · Setting ·
Time · Talk track (marked for pauses) · Objection handling · Close/CTA.**

| ID | Script | Length | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCR-01 | **90-second explainer video script** | 90 sec / ~230 words | Site hero, LinkedIn, conference loop. Storyboarded with the framework animation (DES-05). |
| SCR-02 | **30-second "elevator" version** | 30 sec | Booth, hallway, pre-roll on webinar. |
| SCR-03 | **Board / executive-committee update talk track** | 5–7 min | Board meetings, progress reporting. Includes the platform walkthrough beats. |
| SCR-04 | **Affiliate chapter meeting pitch** | 5 min + 3 min Q&A | The ambassador's core tool. Written so a non-staff volunteer can deliver it cold. |
| SCR-05 | **Conference booth / table script** | 30–60 sec, 3 branches | Branches by who's standing there: deputy, chief, vendor. |
| SCR-06 | **Mentor recruitment call script** | 8–10 min | Warm outbound to retired chiefs. Opens with the ask size, not the vision. |
| SCR-07 | **Voicemail + follow-up email pair** | 20 sec / 80 words | For SCR-06 no-answers. |
| SCR-08 | **Webinar / info-session run of show** | 30 min | 5 welcome · 10 the gap · 8 the framework + live platform demo · 7 Q&A. Includes host lines and a demo checklist. |
| SCR-09 | **Podcast / interview prep sheet** | — | 6 anticipated questions, 3 core messages, 3 bridge phrases, 2 things not to say (dates, price). |
| SCR-10 | **Objection-handling bank (12 objections)** | — | Feeds every script above. Cost, time, "we already do leadership training," "my agency is 12 people," "is this just networking," "why probation-specific." |

**Voice rules for all scripts:** calm, practical, peer-to-peer. Speak to the
weight of the role, never to inadequacy. No hype words (revolutionary,
game-changing, transformational). No claims about outcomes we have not measured.

## 7. Compliance and voice guardrails

- **Prototype status stays visible.** Every asset that links to the site carries
  or leads to the same disclosure the site does: prototype, subject to change and
  Board approval. Do not remove the banner to make a screenshot look cleaner.
- **No unapproved specifics.** No dates, tuition, hours, CEUs, accreditation,
  faculty names, or partner logos until NAPE confirms them in writing (A2).
- **No outcome claims.** Do not say the program improves retention, reduces
  turnover, or produces better leaders. It has not run. Describe design intent.
- **Cite anything external.** If an outreach piece uses a succession or turnover
  statistic, it cites a primary source (BJS, APPA, NIC, a named state report) with
  a link. No unsourced numbers, ever — a wrong statistic in front of chiefs is a
  credibility loss the program cannot afford.
- **Government email realities.** Many recipients are on `.gov`/court systems:
  plain-text-friendly emails, no tracking pixels, no heavy image-only sends, no
  link shorteners (they get filtered). Keep a text-only fallback of every email.
- **PII.** Names/emails collected go only to the Airtable `Interest` table under
  the consent language already on `/connect`. No list sharing, no ad-platform
  uploads, no third-party retargeting pixels. Any analytics must be cookieless
  and disclosed on `/privacy` first (PLAT-06).
- **Tone.** Calm, professional, empathetic. Practical over poetic. Peer voice,
  never vendor voice. The audience runs departments — write to them that way.

## 8. Phased rollout

### Phase 1 — Minimum promotion kit (Weeks 1–2, 9 assets)
Everything needed for one credible announcement that we can measure.

- DES-01 OG images · DES-02 logo lockup · DES-03 framework infographic · DES-04 flyer
- OUT-01 announcement email · OUT-02 nomination email · OUT-10 FAQ
- PLAT-01 OG tags · PLAT-04 UTM + referral capture

**Exit criteria:** a shared link renders a real card; the announcement can go out;
every signup is attributable to a source.

### Phase 2 — Enablement (Weeks 3–6, 13 assets)
Let other people carry it without staff in the room.

- SCR-01 through SCR-05, SCR-10 · OUT-04, OUT-05, OUT-07, OUT-08
- DES-06 slides · DES-07 carousels · DES-08 stage icons
- PLAT-02 `/faq` · PLAT-03 `/toolkit` · PLAT-07 share buttons

**Exit criteria:** an affiliate president can run a 5-minute chapter pitch with
zero NAPE staff support.

### Phase 3 — Depth and scale (Weeks 7–10, 12 assets)
Recruit the supply side and the money side.

- SCR-06 through SCR-09 · OUT-03, OUT-06, OUT-09, OUT-11, OUT-12
- DES-05 explainer video · DES-09 email header · DES-10 conference kit
- DES-11 worksheet · DES-12 certificate refresh
- PLAT-05 `/mentors` · PLAT-06 analytics

**Exit criteria:** mentor pipeline open, sponsor conversation possible, conference-ready.

### Sequencing rules
1. **DES-01/PLAT-01 ship before any announcement.** A blank share card wastes the
   one moment the most people look.
2. **PLAT-04 ships before the first email.** Un-attributed traffic can't be repeated.
3. **Scripts follow OUT-10 (FAQ)** — the objection bank is built from it.
4. **Nothing external ships without the Board/staff review named in §9.**
